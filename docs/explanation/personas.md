# Personas

Agents drive the real Bulwark build as these people, per the fleet testing
rule. Each scenario gives a start state, plain steps, what success looks like,
and what to check. "Standard checks" means: text scale 1.3 at 360 dp width,
dark mode, airplane mode, and every message in plain words. Scenarios aim at
the weak spots found by the September 2026 lens audit.

## Primary: Tariq, a shift-working parent rebuilding a floor

Tariq is 38, a hospital porter on rotating shifts, raising a nine-year-old
with their co-parent across town. Sleep and energy have slipped. They want
two or three habits that survive a chaotic week, not a new regime.

- **Goal:** start a small starter pack and check in every evening in under
  thirty seconds.
- **Context:** checks in at odd hours, often after a night shift, one-handed
  on the bus, text scale 1.3.
- **Would quit if:** a check-in is lost, or the app feels like a compliance
  form.

**T1. Onboarding.** Start: fresh install. Steps: go through onboarding: set
wake and bed times, choose "sleep" as the goal and a slow pace, accept a
starter pack. Success: Continue is not held hostage by an unlabelled
checkbox; the disclaimer reads as standing text; a screen reader names any
checkbox. Check: standard checks.

**T2. Check-in, then back.** Start: two active habits. Steps: open Check-in;
tap "did it" on one and "forgot" on the other; press system back without
tapping Save; reopen Check-in. Success: both answers are still there. Check:
the selected chip is obvious at a glance (a tick, not just a faint tint).

**T3. Why this habit?** Start: Home with active habits. Steps: tap a habit
card to read why it matters. Success: the card opens its detail, or shows the
full reason; no two-line ellipsis with no way in. Check: text scale 1.3.

**T4. Get around.** Start: Home. Steps: go to Library, then to Progress, then
to Settings. Success: each move takes one tap from a labelled bar, not back
and hamburger each time. Check: the current section is marked.

## Secondary: Ngozi, reading progress with low vision

Ngozi is 55, Tariq's aunt, lives with the family, and uses Bulwark at a
table with large text and light mode in a bright kitchen.

- **Goal:** see how the last few weeks went, whether the wall is growing,
  and find one more well-evidenced habit.
- **Context:** low vision, light theme, text scale 1.3.
- **Would quit if:** grey text is unreadable or the chart misleads.

**N1. Read the adherence chart.** Start: one week of check-ins. Steps: open
Progress; read the weekly bars and labels. Success: dates read as "Week of
Sep 9", not "9/14" under "100%"; a rate shows "did of total"; one week does
not fill the whole row. Check: secondary text passes contrast in light and
dark.

**N2. The wall.** Start: two active habits, none graduated. Steps: look at the
wall; tap a stone. Success: the caption matches what is drawn; forming stones
are visible; a screen reader describes the wall. Check: dark mode.

**N3. Filter and add.** Start: Library. Steps: open Filters; pick
"Randomized trials" and a free cost; drag the sheet closed; then apply
properly; add one habit. Success: the evidence tier has one name everywhere
(tag, sheet, Settings); Cost has an "Any" option; the active filter is
visible. Check: after adding, the snackbar offers Undo.
