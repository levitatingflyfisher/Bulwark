A wall between you and entropy. The goal is not perfection — it is a floor that doesn't collapse.

Bulwark is a local-first health-habit app built around graduated habits: you add a stone, the wall holds a little better. Pacing is advisory, never blocking — the app will suggest, and then get out of the way.

• Around 89 interventions, each tagged with the strength of evidence behind it
• A daily check-in that takes seconds
• The drystone Wall — your habits, rendered as stones that stack
• Graduated habits: start where you are, not where a plan says you should be
• Encrypted backup with a 12-word recovery phrase

No ads. No accounts. No analytics. The APK asks for no network permission at all, so it cannot phone home even if it wanted to.

Free and open source, MIT licensed.
